# FSI Market Signals Coworker Walkthrough

1. Ask: “What has changed in the retail banking market recently that might create a marketing opportunity for us?” Start with active market signals. The planted stories include favorable relative savings pricing, aggressive card acquisition offers, and recent mortgage repricing.
2. Ask: “Do we have a meaningful customer opportunity related to that?” The MCP has no customer data. Coworker must switch to Adobe and validate the high-propensity/no-product audiences there.
3. Ask: “How does our proposition compare with the market?” Use `compare_market_propositions` for the category in question. All competitor information is fictional and should be described as demo data.
4. Ask: “Which opportunity should we investigate further and why?” Separate the external signals from Adobe audience evidence. Do not claim the market changes caused propensity or product gaps.
5. Ask: “Develop a campaign brief for the opportunity.” Move into Adobe planning/activation only after the audience and proposition have been investigated. This MCP does not create a campaign or activation artifact.
